PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

The QuickDoc Solutions Engine

An end-to-end ecosystem connecting patients, clinical departments, and moving transit units into one synchronized queue network.

1. Patient Discovery App

A smart mobile interface where users describe symptoms and receive immediate specialty mapping, verified doctor rosters, and guaranteed arrival consultation slots.

2. OP-Flow Hospital Dashboard

A B2B SaaS management cockpit for hospital administrators to balance peak outpatient surges, track specialist availability, and stage digital intake ahead of arrival.

3. Transit Telemetry Pod

Pre-hospital edge hardware linking emergency transit vehicles to receiving hospital triage bays, relaying critical vitals before the patient arrives at the emergency room.

National Health Interoperability (ABDM Ready)

QuickDoc is built to integrate with India’s Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) standards, supporting ABHA identity creation, FHIR-compliant digital records, and seamless interoperability across hospital management software (HIMS).
