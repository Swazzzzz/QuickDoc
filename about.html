OUR MISSION & LEADERSHIP

Built to Eliminate Hospital Guesswork

QuickDoc was founded with a singular purpose: to restore dignity and speed to patient care by replacing blind clinic visits with smart triage routing.

Our Vision

A healthcare world with zero guesswork, zero unmanaged waiting queues, and instant specialist discovery for every patient.

Our Mission

To empower families with direct symptom-based slot booking and equip clinics with automated outpatient flow management.

Our Core Values

Transparency in healthcare discovery, clinical workflow integrity, and accessible health logistics for urban corridors.

Founder: Swathi S

Systems Architect & Venture Lead | Team Admirals

Incubated within the RYLA "Be An Entrepreneur 62.0" cohort (RTS 13.0), Swathi S developed QuickDoc Healthcare Systems to resolve acute outpatient congestion. By combining real-time doctor availability with symptom-driven triage protocols, QuickDoc bridges patients directly to the care they need without transit blindness or lobby delays.
